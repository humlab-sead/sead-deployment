Region review

How this review works

Every SEAD site is linked to several regions (country, län, kommun, socken, landskap…). Many of those regions now have a boundary on the map. This review looks at the cases where a site's coordinates and a region's boundary don't add up, in two steps:

1 · SitesOne card per site, listing all the regions the site is linked to in SEAD, with a ✓ where its coordinates lie inside the region's boundary and a ✗ where they don't. Only the ✗ rows are questions: keep the region, replace it (with a suggestion of the same level where there is one), mark it as wrong, or mark the coordinates as wrong. Regions can be suggested for adding, but nothing is ever added automatically.
2 · RegionsOne card per region concerned, with the evidence from step 1 already applied: sites moved away or with wrong coordinates no longer count, and sites confirmed as belonging to the region do, even if they lie just outside the boundary. You decide whether the region is linked to its boundary on the map.

Nothing in SEAD changes while you review. Your answers become a change request that is reviewed in a pull request (decisions D-21, D-22, D-24).

Where the map information comes from: parishes and towns from Lantmäteriet; kommuner, län and foreign regions from GADM (coarse coastlines); landskap are our own construction from Lantmäteriet's parishes, so they are only suggested where the parish's landskap is known (Wikidata or a hand-made assignment), never where it was guessed.
