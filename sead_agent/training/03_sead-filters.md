# SEAD filters

Every filter this deployment offers, generated from `https://sead.local/query/api/facets` on 2026-10-05. The id is what
`add_filter`, `set_filter_selections` and `remove_filter` take; it is not guessable from the
title. `list_filters` is more current, but covers only the active domain - this list is the
only place that says what the other domains offer.

## Domains

`general` offers every filter; each other domain offers a subset, and asking for a filter
the active domain lacks fails.
Switching domain **removes** the filters it lacks, so "dendro data in Småland" is best
served by staying in `general` and adding `dataset_methods` (pick the dendrochronology
method) alongside `region` - the `dendrochronology` domain has no way to filter by region.
Switch domain only when the user asks for one, or when every filter you need exists in it.

## The filters

One line each: id, title, type, description, and the domains beyond `general` that offer it.
A filter with no type is `discrete`: its selections are integer ids from `get_filter_options`.
`range` and `rangesintersect` take `[lower, upper]`.

### Ecology

- `abundance_classification` abundance classification - Type of quantification or classification system used to record presence or abundance of organisms or material properties. *`general` only*
- `abundances_all` Abundances (range) - Quantification of the amount (number, presence etc.) of an organism (taxon, species etc.). *also palaeoentomology, archaeobotany, pollen*
- `ecocode` Eco code (staged: `ecocode_system` then `ecocode`) - Ecological category (trait) or cultural relevance of organisms based on a classification system. *also palaeoentomology, archaeobotany, pollen*

### Measured values

- `tbl_denormalized_measured_values_32` Loss on Ignition (range) - Organic content in % from weight lost when heated to a high temperature (e.g. 550°C). *also geoarchaeology*
- `tbl_denormalized_measured_values_33_0` Magnetic sus. (range) - Measure of the degree to which a material can be magnetized in the presence of an external magnetic field. *also geoarchaeology*
- `tbl_denormalized_measured_values_33_82` MS Heating 550 (range) - Measurement of the magnetic susceptibility of a sample after heating at a temperature of 550°C. *also geoarchaeology*
- `tbl_denormalized_measured_values_37` Phosphates (range) - Concentration of phosphate compounds in a sample, often used as an indicator of past human activity. *also geoarchaeology*

### Others

- `abundance_elements` Abundance Elements - The part (element) of the organism (plant or animal) that was counted. *also archaeobotany, pollen*
- `tbl_biblio_modern` Bibliography modern - Published references associated with species descriptions or taxonomic identification literature. *all domains*
- `tbl_biblio_sites` Bibliography sites - Publications and reports directly associated with excavation sites. *`general` only*
- `tbl_biblio_sample_groups` Bibliography sites/Samplegroups - Publications and reports associated with specific excavation sites or sample groups. *`general` only*
- `construction_purpose` Construction purpose - The intended function of a dated timber construction (e.g. residential, religious, infrastructure, naval). *also dendrochronology*
- `constructions` Constructions - Dated timber constructions identified through dendrochronological analysis (e.g. buildings, bridges, ships). *`general` only*
- `data_types` Data types - Types of data generated by an analysis or observation. *all domains*
- `feature_type` Feature type - Archaeological, geological, environmental, or cultural features associated with the excavation and samples. *all domains*
- `modification_types` Modification Types - Types of modification to an organism (insect, seed, bone etc.), such as it being a fragment or carbonised. *also archaeobotany*
- `record_types` Proxy types - General type of proxy measurement (e.g. pollen, dating) used to infer aspects of the past. *`general` only*
- `rdb_codes` RDB Code - Red Data Book codes provide an description of the conservation status of a species in a particular geographical area. *also palaeoentomology*
- `rdb_systems` RDB system - Red Data Book systems are used to classify and document the conservation status of species and ecosystems. *also palaeoentomology*
- `sample_group_sampling_contexts` Sampling Contexts - The context in which the sample was collected. *all domains*

### Space/Time

- `analysis_entity_ages` Timeline (rangesintersect) - Age range assigned to analysis entities based on dating evidence; uses range-intersection matching. *`general` only*
- `country` Countries - The name of the country, at the time of collection, in which the samples were collected. *all domains*
- `dataset_methods` Dataset methods - The various methods and techniques used for creating, collecting or analyzing the data. *`general` only*
- `dataset_provider` Dataset provider - The institution or individual responsible for contributing a dataset to SEAD. *`general` only*
- `datasets` Datasets. *all domains*
- `dendro_age_contained_by` Dendrochronology ages (rangesintersect) - Age range filter for dendrochronology, covering both estimated felling year and outermost tree ring date. *also dendrochronology*
- `geochronology` Geochronology (range) - Sample ages as retrieved through absolute methods such as radiocarbon dating or other radiometric methods (in method based years before present - e.g. 14C years). *also palaeoentomology, archaeobotany, pollen, geoarchaeology, dendrochronology, ceramic*
- `activeseason` Insect activity seasons - Season in which an adult insect has been observed to be active. *also palaeoentomology, archaeobotany, pollen*
- `location_types` Location type - Administrative level of a geographic location entry (e.g. country, region, municipality). *all domains*
- `region` Region - Modern or historical geographical or administrative region. *`general` only*
- `sample_groups` Sample groups - A collection of samples, usually defined by the excavator or collector. *all domains*
- `sites` Site - General name for the excavation or sampling location. *all domains*
- `sites_polygon` Map polygon (polygons, set with `set_map_polygons`). *all domains*
- `relative_age_name` Time periods - Age of sample as defined by association with a (often regionally specific) cultural or geological period (in years before present). *all domains*

### Taxonomy

- `species_author` Author - Authority of the taxonomic name (not used for all species). *also palaeoentomology, archaeobotany, pollen, dendrochronology*
- `family` Family - Taxonomic family. *also palaeoentomology, archaeobotany, pollen, dendrochronology*
- `genus` Genus - Taxonomic genus (under family). *also palaeoentomology, archaeobotany, pollen, dendrochronology*
- `species` Taxa - Taxonomic species (under genus). *also palaeoentomology, archaeobotany, pollen, dendrochronology*

## Method abbreviations

The site report's Samples table, the overview charts and the Table view's Analyses column
name methods by these short forms, with the full name only in a tooltip you cannot read:

`AAR` Amino Acid Racemization; `aDNA-Library` Ancient DNA analysis (Library Preparation); `aDNA-Sample` Ancient DNA analysis (Sample Extraction/Analysis); `ArchCal` Other archaeological dating; `ArchPer` Archaeological period (unspecified); `Auger sample` Soil auger or probe; `C14 AMS` C14 Accelerator dating; `C14 Humous` C14 dating of humic substances in sediment; `C14 Std` C14 Conventional; `C14 Unspec.` Radiocarbon (14C Unspecified); `CalAMS` Calibrated AMS radiocarbon date; `citPtot` Total phosphates citric acid; `ESR` Electron Spin Resonance; `Fossil insects` Palaeoentomology; `GeolCal` Geologically derived calendar date; `GeolPer` Geological period (unspecified years); `Grain size 0,6-2mm (%)` 0,6mm (%); `HistCal` Historical Records; `HistPer` Historical Period; `LOI` Loss on ignition; `MS` Magnetic susceptibility; `MS-hi` Magnetic susceptibility (High frequency); `MS-lo` Magnetic susceptibility (Low frequency); `Plant macros` Plant macrofossil analyses; `Pollen` Palynology; `P°HCL` Phosphate degrees HCL; `Russian core` Core by Russian; `Strat(Geol)` Stratigraphic (Geological); `Temp record` Temporary record; `TephraCal` Tephrochronology Calendar; `TL` Thermoluminescence; `TypoCal` Artefact typology (calendar years); `U-Series` Uranium-Series (general); `U234/U238` U-Series U234/U238; `UnknownCal` Calendar date by unknown method; `UnknownOther` Other date by unknown method; `UnknownRadio` Radiometric date by unknown method.
